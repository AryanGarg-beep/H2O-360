Community Water Intelligence Platform

Built to empower communities, planners, and local governments with real-time monitoring, transparent forecasting, and explainable water analytics.

Who this Is Built For

H2O-360° is designed as a decision-support system for government bodies, public utilities, and planners, while remaining rooted in community-level data.

🏛 Government & Local Bodies

Bridges gaps in delayed, incomplete, or fragmented datasets by generating explainable forecasts and ward- or village-level insights aligned with public reporting structures.

🚰 Water Utilities

Enables monitoring of flow, detection of anomalies, and demand forecasting to support allocation, leakage reduction, and infrastructure maintenance.

📊 Planners & Agencies

Supports medium- and long-term planning using transparent models grounded in seasonal patterns, historical trends, and real-world sensor data.

🌱 Communities

Captures on-ground realities that often never reach centralized systems, strengthening bottom-up visibility without requiring technical expertise.
